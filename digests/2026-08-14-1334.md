## ★ 1. Luna-TTS Family Technical Report — `2608.11593` — **Relevance 10/10 · Quality 9/10**
*Feng Yin, Shuai Shi, Junjie Zheng, Kechenying Zhou, Yiqiu Wang, Chenyang He, Qiuhua Jiang, Mengxiao Bi, Yanmin Qian, Bing Han, Chenda Li, Wangyou Zhang, Xun Gong … and 9 others* — **VUI Labs Research** · cs.SD, eess.AS · 2026-08-12 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11593)

*Problem:* AR codec LMs own modern TTS but pay for it — latency that grows with utterance length, error accumulation down the committed prefix, and a left-to-right order imposed on an RVQ grid that has no natural one.
*Method:* A diffusion-language-model TTS pretrained on 1M hours of zh/en/ja/ko, built by progressively adapting a pretrained AR text LLM from causal → bidirectional → block-causal attention over a 0.6B backbone, plus annealed fine-tuning for emotion and non-verbal vocalizations and a GRPO stage over the realized denoising trajectory.
*Result:* Best on all four Seed-TTS-Eval metrics against open and commercial systems (0.73 CER / 79.7 SIM test-zh; 1.49 WER / 76.8 SIM test-en), lowest error rates on in-the-wild CV3-Eval, and a Realtime variant at 0.0240 RTF / 41.6 ms first-block latency.

**Why it ranked:** ★ Followed author Yanmin Qian, and the most on-profile paper of the window by a distance — it sits on the TTS, codec-LM and zero-shot-cloning axes at once. The quality call is about scope and follow-through: 1M hours, a full AR→bidirectional→block-causal adaptation curriculum, an RL stage, *and* both a non-AR and a streaming variant sharing one tokenizer and backbone, with sweeps across all four Seed-TTS-Eval metrics rather than a cherry-picked pair. Held at 9 rather than 10 only because it is a technical report with no code release stated, so the 1M-hour data pipeline stays a black box.

## ★ 2. MiDashengLM-Gen: Unified Audio Scene Generation via LLM-Driven Autoregressive Flow Matching — `2608.11804` — **Relevance 9/10 · Quality 8/10**
*Xingwei Sun, Heinrich Dinkel, Gang Li, Jiahao Mei, Yadong Niu, Zerui Han, Yuepeng Jiang, Jiahao Zhou, Lichun Fan, Jian Luan* — **MiLM Plus, Xiaomi Inc. & X-LANCE Lab, Shanghai Jiao Tong University** · eess.AS, cs.SD · 2026-08-12 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11804)

*Problem:* Unified audio generators that blend speech, music and sound effects run a disjointed pipeline — a frozen text encoder feeding a separate audio decoder — which blocks cross-modal optimization and leaves embedded speech barely intelligible.
*Method:* An end-to-end model coupling a pretrained LLM with per-token conditional flow matching, giving autoregressive, variable-length mixed-audio scene generation from one trained stack rather than an encoder-decoder handoff.
*Result:* English WER on Seed-TTS falls 12.15% → 2.79%, closing most of the gap to dedicated TTS (1.24%), with competitive multilingual WERs and mixed-audio quality held on MECAT. Code, checkpoints and demo all public.

**Why it ranked:** ★ Followed lab (SJTU X-LANCE, with Xiaomi's MiDasheng team), and the intelligibility number is the one that matters — a 4.4× WER cut is the difference between "unified audio model" as a demo and as something you would actually put speech through. Quality at 8 because the contribution is a clean architectural simplification with an honest reference point (it names the 1.24% dedicated-TTS ceiling it *doesn't* reach) and a full open release; docked from higher because the mixed-audio side is defended as "competitive" rather than won, so the unification still costs something.

## 3. VoxAudio: Vocalized Audio Synthesis via Multi-Reward Autoregressive Flow Matching — `2608.12951` — **Relevance 9/10 · Quality 8/10**
*Wenxiang Guo, Changhao Pan, Ziyue Jiang, Fei Wu, Zhou Zhao* — **Zhejiang University** · cs.SD · 2026-08-13 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12951)

*Problem:* Text-to-audio systems asked for speech inside a soundscape either reduce it to unintelligible murmur or farm it out to a separate TTS and mix post-hoc, forfeiting control over *when* speech occurs and how it interacts with the scene.
*Method:* A causal AR flow-matching model with chunk-wise causal factorization and independent per-chunk noise levels for KV-cached sliding-window streaming, pretrained with randomized chunk boundaries; multi-reward Negative-aware FineTuning jointly optimizes semantic fidelity, linguistic accuracy, aesthetics and temporal grounding; plus VoxCorpus and the interval-annotated VoxBench.
*Result:* WER 0.038 on VoxBench-10s against a 0.264 baseline, 1.61% WER on Seed-TTS-Eval, temporal-grounding IoU 0.654, MOS-Overall 4.47, streaming at 0.32 RTF.

**Why it ranked:** The second unified speech-in-soundscape generator in 48 hours — that convergence is itself the signal, and VoxAudio attacks the axis MiDashengLM-Gen doesn't: streaming and explicit temporal placement. Building VoxCorpus and VoxBench to supply the missing verbatim-transcript-with-intervals supervision is the real contribution, since the task was previously unmeasurable. Quality held at 8 rather than higher because the headline 0.038-vs-0.264 gap is measured on the authors' own new benchmark; the Seed-TTS-Eval number is the externally comparable one, and it is good but not leading.

## ★ 4. The SLT 2026 SmartGlasses Challenge: Benchmarking Egocentric Multi-Talker Speech Recognition and Understanding with Audio-Language Models — `2608.12034` — **Relevance 8/10 · Quality 7/10**
*Dehui Gao, Zhixian Zhao, Zhennan Lin, Yujie Liao, Yuhang Dai, Yike Zhu, Longshuai Xiao, Hui Bu, Xin Xu, Xie Chen, Shuai Wang, Liumeng Xue, Zhonghua Fu, Jun Du, Eng-Siong Chng, Jun Zhou, Lei Xie* — **ASLP@NPU (Northwestern Polytechnical University), Shanghai Jiao Tong University, USTC, Nanjing University, NTU Singapore, Huawei, AIShell & Rokid** · eess.AS, cs.SD · 2026-08-12 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12034)

*Problem:* Smart glasses put a microphone array on continuous egocentric duty, where dynamic acoustics, speaker overlap and wearer-centred recording geometry break both recognition and understanding — and there was no shared benchmark for it.
*Method:* Two tracks (Dyadic Dialogue, Multi-party Meeting) jointly scoring Time-Stamped Speaker-Attributed ASR and spoken language understanding, on a new 106-hour four-channel egocentric corpus of 714 real-world sessions; 15 teams submitted.
*Result:* Best system cut tcpCER 18.67% → 5.23% (dyadic) and 57.81% → 27.95% (multi-party), SLU accuracy 69.9% → 88.8% and 65.9% → 93.0%; overlap above 15% still pushes mean tcpCER from 8.6% to 14.2%.

**Why it ranked:** ★ Followed author Xie Chen and followed lab SJTU X-LANCE, on a resource that is immediately actionable — 106 hours of genuinely multi-channel egocentric conversation is scarce data, and speaker-attributed ASR plus SLU on one corpus is the setup audio-LLM work keeps needing. The finding worth carrying: systems are near-solved on moderate-overlap dyadic audio (5.23% tcpCER) but still collapse on long multi-party meetings at 27.95%, and are weakest on *acoustic-reasoning* questions — paralinguistics remains the open gap, not transcription. Quality at 7 because a challenge summary reports others' systems rather than advancing a method.

## 5. Rethinking Language Model-Based Generative Speech Enhancement in the Latent Space of a Neural Audio Codec — `2608.12082` — **Relevance 8/10 · Quality 8/10**
*Yihui Fu, Zhengyang Li, Tim Fingscheidt* — **Technische Universität Braunschweig, Germany** (compute via the German AI Service Center WestAI) · eess.AS · 2026-08-12 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12082)

*Problem:* LM-based speech enhancement over neural-audio-codec latents has proliferated into half a dozen incompatible paradigms, each published against its own data, codec and metric set — so nobody knows which design choice is actually carrying the result.
*Method:* A unified framework covering all six — discrete/continuous autoregressive, discrete/continuous non-autoregressive, discrete diffusion and continuous flow matching — trained and evaluated identically on URGENT 2025 splits with both intrusive and non-intrusive metrics, plus a proposed auxiliary-loss fine-tuning stage on reconstructed speech.
*Result:* Every continuous-domain paradigm beats its discrete counterpart; continuous non-autoregressive wins overall (DNSMOS 3.03, NISQA 3.41, PESQ 3.00), and the auxiliary-loss fine-tuning lifts DNSMOS, NISQA, PESQ and POLQA consistently across all six.

**Why it ranked:** Accepted at IWAENC 2026 and the highest-rigor paper in the window — the kind of controlled six-way comparison that saves the field a year of incommensurable single-paradigm papers. "Continuous beats discrete" is a clean, portable finding that bears directly on the tokenizer-versus-continuous-latent question running through the TTS entries above, so it earns its slot on more than speech enhancement alone. Held at 8 because it is a study rather than a new capability, and everything rests on one codec and one challenge's data splits.

### Honorable mentions
- ★ **RT-SEMamba: Real-Time Speech Enhancement Mamba via Progressive Knowledge Distillation** — `2608.12099` — Academia Sinica & National Taiwan University (Rong Chao, Yu Tsao), Kore University of Enna & University of Palermo (Moreno La Quatra, Sabato Marco Siniscalchi), and **NVIDIA** (Szu-Wei Fu). Fully causal time-frequency Mamba blocks propagate a fixed-size recurrent state instead of a growing KV cache, and progressive KD compresses an 8-layer teacher into a 1-layer student by distilling complex spectral outputs *and* intermediate representations. 3.32 PESQ at a 25 ms latency constraint on Voicebank-DEMAND; the distilled student goes 3.06 → 3.18 PESQ at unchanged steady-state RTF for a 2.75× speedup. Interspeech 2026. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12099)
- **Alignment Drift in Single-Model Speculative Decoding for ASR: Mechanism, Correction, and Cost** — `2608.12703` — Xinyu Wang, Huapeng Zhou, Ziyu Zhao, Silin Meng, Ke Bai, Dongming Shen, Xiao-Wen Chang, **Alex Smola** (no affiliation block on the preprint; Chang is McGill, Smola is Boson AI). The sharpest diagnosis here: a lightweight ASR draft head can read the whole audio at every step yet still degrades as it runs, because *access is not localization* — it loses the audio position while the accepted text keeps the transcript position explicit. Late-draft median error reaches 21 frames where target attention during verification stays within 2. Per-step audio access roughly doubles later-proposal acceptance, and correctly placed fixed windows recover continuation while equally narrow misplaced ones destroy it. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12703)
- **On-Policy Self-Distillation for Multi-Dialect ASR: Mastering Dialects, Retaining Mandarin** — `2608.11898` — ASLP@NPU (Lei Xie), WeNet Community (Binbin Zhang) & Nexdata. Trains the student on its own decoded prefixes while a frozen teacher, conditioned on the reference transcript as privileged context, supplies soft token-level targets — replacing hard cross-entropy on dialect data and so sidestepping the usual dialect-gains-for-Mandarin-losses trade. On Qwen3-ASR-1.7B: dialect CER 15.37 → 12.79 *and* Mandarin 3.46 → 3.27, improving all eight Mandarin sets rather than trading them away. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11898)
- **Phoenix TTS: High-Fidelity Synthesis and Voice Conversion via Flow-Matching-Driven Speech Tokenization** — `2608.11737` — DiDi (L-Lab Phoenix-Audio Team). Attacks the same tokenizer bottleneck as ReLMCodec did on 08-12, from the other end: instead of measuring predictability, it closes the loop, optimizing the speech tokenizer to reconstruct SSL features *while* receiving direct supervision from the downstream flow-matching acoustic model — removing the feature gap between discrete tokens and the continuous space the generator wants. The clearest statement yet that ASR-objective tokenizers cap speaker similarity by construction. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11737)
- **In Defense of Using Worst-case Privacy Disclosure as Privacy Evaluation Metric of Voice Anonymization** — `2608.10318` — Xin Wang (NII, Yamagishi lab) & Xiaoxiao Miao (SIT). No new metric — a careful argument that EER, the community default, can rate a system "ideal" while individual speakers still leak in log-likelihood-ratio space, and that the rank-based metric reduces to the same perfect-secrecy principle as privacy-ZEBRA. Also shows how the LLR estimation method itself moves the evaluation. Notebook released; SPSC 2026. Worth reading if you evaluate anonymization or anti-spoofing. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.10318)
- **Do Text-to-Music Models Really Follow Instructions? A Counterfactual Evaluation of Key and Beat Grouping** — `2608.11899` — Yining Wang, solo. Prompted-attribute agreement is the standard controllability evidence, and this shows it is mostly measuring the base rate: a matched counterfactual design with a neutral input and two swapped targets, shared seed, frozen native adapters. Stable Audio 3 produces four-beat grouping in 0.97 of *neutral* cases but only 0.56 when four-beat is explicitly requested. ACE-Step 1.5 and Stable Audio 3 show real key control; LeVo2 does not. Off-attribute placebos and blind expert annotation back it up. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11899)
- **Dialogue-Aware Video-to-Music Generation Using Public Domain Film Collections** — `2608.11576` — UC San Diego (Haven Kim, Zachary Novack, Julian McAuley) & University of Michigan (Hao-Wen Dong). OSSL-v2: 34,343 clips / 246.4 hours from public-domain films, self-hosted and copyright-conscious, built specifically against the link-rot that makes YouTube-URL music corpora unreproducible. Then uses it to study dialogue as a conditioning signal, adding a time axis to video cross-attention and modulating it frame-by-frame with the dialogue track. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11576)
- **The GENEA Challenge 2026: A Large-Scale Disentangled Evaluation of Speech-Driven Gesture Generation** — `2608.10839` — KTH (Rajmund Nagy, Gustav Eje Henter), Taras Kucherenko, Youngwoo Yoon et al. Off-profile as a topic, included for the evaluation methodology: four user studies, 23,000+ votes from 869 test-takers, disentangling motion quality from speech alignment plus a dyadic mismatching study to isolate reacting-to-the-interlocutor. Sobering headline — filtered dataset segments beat every submission at 68–95% pairwise winrate, and the top system scores 32% speech alignment against a 62% mocap ceiling. A template for how to run a generative-audio human evaluation honestly. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.10839)
