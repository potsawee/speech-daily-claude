## ★ 1. GROW: Group-Relative Advantage-Weighted On-Policy Reinforcement Learning of Autoregressive-Diffusion Text-to-Speech model — `2608.03215` — **Relevance 9/10 · Quality 9/10**
*Guanrou Yang, Tian Tan, Qian Chen, Ziyang Ma, Yakun Song, Zhikang Niu, Qi Chen, Wenming Tu, Haitao Li, Shan Yang, Xie Chen* — **SJTU X-LANCE, Zhejiang University, Alibaba Group & Zhejiang Lab** · eess.AS, cs.AI, cs.CL · 2026-08-04 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03215)

*Problem:* RL for flow-matching TTS normally forces the deterministic ODE sampler into an SDE and tracks per-step likelihood ratios, injecting noise and heavy compute.
*Method:* Reweight the standard flow-matching regression loss directly with group-relative advantages from separately standardized intelligibility and speaker-similarity rewards, anchored by a Wasserstein-2 velocity penalty to a frozen reference.
*Result:* On DiTAR, WER 2.016 → 1.558 and speaker similarity 0.676 → 0.715 with UTMOS held, training 2.9× faster than 32-NFE DiTAR-GRPO.

**Why it ranked:** ★ Followed author Xie Chen (SJTU X-LANCE) with Alibaba — a clean, well-motivated fix to a real pain point in RL-for-TTS, backed by concrete WER/SIM gains, a speed win, an honest analysis of why signed advantages beat exponential weighting, and a promise to release code, a faithful DiTAR reproduction and all checkpoints.

## ★ 2. Qwen-Audio-3.0-Gen-Preview Technical Report — `2607.27011` — **Relevance 10/10 · Quality 8/10**
*Junyu Dai, Xiaoyue Duan, Xinyue Fan, Yihan Feng, Jingbei Li, Xiangang Li, Yunjia Li, Lejun Min, Yufei Shi, Xingchen Song, Yiran Wang, Cheng Wen, Menglin Wu, Bajian Xiang, Huaicheng Zhang, Han Zhao, Ruichen Zheng* — **Alibaba Group (Qwen Team)** · eess.AS · 2026-07-29 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.27011)

*Problem:* Existing single-domain audio systems cannot organize heterogeneous components — speech, ambience, multiple roles — into one long-form temporal scene.
*Method:* A unified non-autoregressive Diffusion Transformer over a shared continuous VAE that compresses 48 kHz stereo into 25 Hz semantically supervised latents, with prompt enhancement turning free-form requests into structured temporal records.
*Result:* Clearest strengths in speaker similarity, cross-turn consistency across both languages, and temporal localization; AudioCaps gains concentrate under LALM and AudioBox judging.

**Why it ranked:** ★ Followed lab (Alibaba/Qwen) previewing the generation half of the Qwen-Audio 3.0 stack — about as high-relevance as a preprint gets for this profile, and the single-representation-for-all-audio design is the direction the field is converging on; capped at 8 on quality because it is a preview report with hedged "clearest comparative strength" framing rather than clean SOTA margins or a released checkpoint.

## ★ 3. Voice Memory for Agentic Speech Recognition — `2607.26410` — **Relevance 9/10 · Quality 8/10**
*Chao-Han Huck Yang, Zih-Ching Chen, Piotr Zelasko, Zhehuai Chen, Jagadeesh Balam, Boris Ginsburg* — **NVIDIA** · cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.SD, eess.AS · 2026-07-29 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.26410)

*Problem:* Generative error correction over ASR hypotheses over-corrects, breaking tokens that were already right — on financial news, up to 64% of its edits.
*Method:* An inference-only listener–thinker split: a frozen corrector reads one per-domain `memory.md` and decides per utterance whether to edit or abstain, while an asynchronous score-gated optimizer rewrites that file through bounded, held-out-verified edits. No weights change.
*Result:* Weighted WER 8.36% → 7.52% across ten HyPoradise domains with no dataset regressing below 1-best; air-travel 8.40% → 3.40%, CHiME-4 12.69% → 10.46%.

**Why it ranked:** ★ Two followed authors at NVIDIA (Chao-Han Huck Yang, Boris Ginsburg); a genuinely novel zero-parameter agentic-memory framing whose headline finding — that the skill the loop actually learns is restraint — is measured honestly rather than glossed, and the memory plus code are openly released.

## ★ 4. Stable Autoregressive Speech Generation with Low-Frame-Rate High-Dimensional Continuous Tokens — `2607.29363` — **Relevance 9/10 · Quality 8/10**
*Yi Luo, Rongzhi Gu, Jixun Yao* — **ByteDance Seed & Columbia University** · eess.AS, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.SD · 2026-07-31 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.29363)

*Problem:* Autoregressive speech generation trades sequence length against representational capacity — richer or faster-rate tokens keep detail but amplify drift and error accumulation over long horizons.
*Method:* Locodec, a locally encoded codec shaped so a low-dimensional core manifold stays interpolatable while native high-dimensional coordinates remain identifiable, paired with MP-ELD, a single-token AR flow-matching generator using multi-path routing and residual classifier-free guidance.
*Result:* At 8 Hz and 768 dimensions it holds reconstruction quality, improves single-token predictability, reaches competitive WER and stays stable on long-form synthesis.

**Why it ranked:** ★ Followed lab (ByteDance Seed), with Yi Luo (Conv-TasNet, DPRNN) attacking the tokenizer↔generator co-design problem sitting at the centre of the profile's codec and speech-LM interests; the standout claim is that it needs no external SSL/ASR model, no pretrained text LM and no post-training stage — held at 8 only because the abstract reports "competitive" rather than headline numbers.

## 5. ParaASR: Multi-Token Prediction for Fast and Long-Context LLM-Based Speech Recognition — `2607.29279` — **Relevance 9/10 · Quality 8/10**
*Qingjian Lin, Yuxin Li, Haoyang Zhang, Jun Chen, Yechang Huang, Feng Tian, Xie Li, Xiangyu Tony Zhang, Daijiao Liu, Yuxin Zhang, Jinglan Gong, Bo Zhao, Fei Tian, Xuerui Yang, Gang Yu, Xiangyu Zhang, Daxin Jiang* — **StepFun**, with NTU, Peking University, UNSW, SJTU & USTC · cs.SD · 2026-07-31 · [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.29279)

*Problem:* In encoder-LLM ASR, autoregressive decoding cost scales with decoder size, forcing a hard trade-off between transcription quality and serving latency.
*Method:* Multi-token prediction lets a 4B LLM decoder propose a six-token continuation per forward step via five aligned future-token branches trained by a staged recipe, admitting only the autoregressively verified prefix.
*Result:* 5.0 of 6 proposed tokens accepted on average, RTF down to 0.0053, a native 32K window transcribing 30 minutes in one pass, and 2.97% / 3.68% / 3.70% average error on Chinese, English and long-form sets.

**Why it ranked:** Squarely in the streaming, long-context LLM-ASR territory the profile prizes, resting on a sharp and correct insight — because ASR output is anchored to the acoustic signal, multi-token acceptance is far higher than in open-ended generation — with hard latency *and* accuracy numbers from a credible frontier lab.

### Honorable mentions
- **LILAC: An Idempotent Neural Speech Codec** — `2608.05727` — Seoul National University (Sungroh Yoon's group). A 24 kHz codec at 9.375 Hz / 0.75 kbit/s that is idempotent *by construction* — re-encoding its own decoded audio returns the identical token stream — where all twelve baselines rewrite ≥15% of tokens per round trip, at competitive UTMOS 4.14 / 4.24. Narrow but genuinely new property, and it matters for editing pipelines. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05727)

F5-TTS extended to disentangle speech, noise and reverberation with separate speaker/environment prompts and triple classifier-free guidance; TASLP submission, so a journal extension rather than fresh news. XML-tagged, transcript-grounded edit instructions over a continuous AR foundation model covering text, emotion, prosody and pause edits, plus the bilingual doteBench suite. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02673)

A training-free decoding fix that cuts WER/CER by up to 55.6% across all SeedTTS-Eval settings and 24 of 25 CV3-Eval settings, with +0.644 CMOS. Unusually strong for a zero-training intervention. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.00722)

Per-sample rubrics synthesized from the raw waveform and regenerated against the model's own rollouts, so the process reward keeps chasing the current policy's weaknesses instead of saturating. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.02831)
- ★ **Teffic-Audio: Tell Fact from Fiction** — `2607.28351` — Zhizheng Wu (Teffic Labs / CUHK-Shenzhen). A deliberately plain Conformer + attentive-stats-pooling detector that wins on recipe rather than architecture: pooled EER 1.454% over Speech-DF-Arena's 14 test sets, best public system on the leaderboard, trained only on open data. [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.28351)
